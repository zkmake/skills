# Kansas (KS)

In effect for 2026–27: the **2017 Kansas Mathematics Standards**, adopted by the Kansas State Board of Education in August 2017 (the year they reached classrooms is not recorded). They are Common Core revised, written without cluster letters. Mathness models them as a crosswalk in Kansas's codes: edition `ks`, 149 K–5 rows, page `/kansas/`. The Mathness edition note reads "Kansas teaches Common Core's grade 4 angle measures in grade 8." KSDE was not found to have adopted newer math standards, so there is no next edition.

## Codes
- `Grade.Domain.Number` with **no cluster letter**. Lettered sub-parts are appended: K.CC.4a, 2.MD.9, 3.MD.8d.
- Domains are CCSS's: CC (K), OA, NBT, NF (3–5), MD, G.
- Sub-parts are folded into their parent. The exceptions are sub-parts that carry new Kansas content or split-off CCSS content; these keep their own rows: K.CC.4d, 1.NBT.2d, 2.NBT.1c. 1.NBT.4 is split into 4a–4c in the document; Mathness folds them.
- **Renumbering, so never match by number:**
  - 2.MD.9 is new (coins and bills), so CCSS 2.MD.9 and 2.MD.10 became KS 2.MD.10 and 2.MD.11.
  - CCSS 3.MD.2 was split into KS 3.MD.2 and 3.MD.3, so CCSS 3.MD.3–3.MD.8 became KS 3.MD.4–3.MD.9.
  - Grade 4 MD stops at 4.MD.4, because the angle-measure standards are gone.
  - Same-shaped codes elsewhere mean other content: Alaska's 2.MD.9 is data, and MGSE2.MD.9 (Georgia's old codes) is the length line plot.
- Rows by grade: K 23, 1 22, 2 28, 3 26, 4 25, 5 25.

## Against Common Core
- 149 rows: 134 same, 15 edited, 0 moved, 0 new.
- CCSS dropped from K–5:
  - **4.MD.C.5, 4.MD.C.6, 4.MD.C.7** (angle concepts, protractor, additive angles) move to Kansas grade 8 (8.G.1–8.G.3).
  - **5.OA.B.3** (two numerical patterns, ordered pairs) is removed; KSDE says it's covered in 6.RP.3a.
  - Partly dropped: brackets and braces in 5.OA.A.1; a.m./p.m. in 2.MD.C.7 (moved to KS 3.MD.1).
- K: counting to 100 as a growing pattern (K.CC.1).
- Grade 1:
  - Growing and repeating patterns in counting to 120 (1.NBT.1).
  - Flexible tens and ones, such as 20 as 1 ten and 10 ones (1.NBT.2d).
- Grade 2:
  - Flexible hundreds, tens and ones, such as 207 as 20 tens 7 ones (2.NBT.1c).
  - Skip-count by 2s as well (2.NBT.2).
  - Time to five minutes without a.m./p.m. (2.MD.7).
  - **Recognize coins and bills and their values** (2.MD.9; edited, served from 2.MD.C.8).
- Grade 3: time to the minute **with a.m. and p.m.**, plus elapsed time (3.MD.1, mapped to 3.MD.A.1 and 2.MD.C.7).
- Grade 4:
  - **Straight and reflex angles** (4.G.1).
  - Triangles sorted by angles and by sides (4.G.2).
  - Fraction data in bar graphs or pictographs as well as line plots (4.MD.4).
  - Any efficient algorithm for adding and subtracting (4.NBT.4).
- Grade 5:
  - Parentheses only (5.OA.1).
  - Partial products or the standard algorithm (5.NBT.5).
  - **Sixteenths** in line plots, bar graphs or pictographs (5.MD.2).
- The not-equal symbol was added to comparison standards (1.NBT.3, 2.NBT.4, 3.NF.3d, 4.NBT.2, 4.NF.2, 4.NF.7, 5.NBT.3b). Fluency is worded "efficiently, accurately, and flexibly". Both are treated as same.
- Judgment calls:
  - KSDE's comparison says 4.G.1 has "NO CHANGES", but the 2017 text adds straight and reflex angles, so it's marked edited.
  - The splits (K.CC.3 / K.CC.4d; 3.MD.2 / 3.MD.3; 1.NBT.4a–c) are marked same because no content changed.

## Sheets for state content
- OVR is empty: Kansas adds no new or moved rows, so every row is served by its CCSS code's sheets.
- Fit-review links (extra.tsv):
  - Coins and bills: 2.MD.9 + 1.GR.C.6 (Maryland's coin and bill sheet).
  - Skip counting: 2.NBT.2 + 2.NOS.A.2.
  - Angles and shapes: 4.G.1 + 4.MD.C.5; 4.G.2 + 4.G.A.1 and 5.MG.3 (Virginia triangles).
  - Data: 5.MD.2 + 4.MD.A.2.
- In earlier fit passes, 2.NBT.2 (skip-counting by 2s), 4.G.2 (triangle types), 4.G.1 (reflex angles) and 5.MD.2 (sixteenths in a line plot) were partial. All were rated good in later passes, and none remain.
- No sheets were written for Kansas alone.
