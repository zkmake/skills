# Indiana (IN)

In effect for 2026–27: the **Indiana Academic Standards for Mathematics**, adopted by the Indiana SBOE in June 2023, taught since 2024–25, and assessed by ILEARN from 2025–26. They are Indiana's own standards with their own codes, and they cut a good deal of Common Core. Mathness models them as a crosswalk: edition `in`, 122 K–5 rows, page `/indiana/`. No next edition.

## Documents
- IDOE mathematics standards page: https://www.in.gov/doe/students/indiana-academic-standards/mathematics/
- Standards PDFs, one per grade: https://media.doe.in.gov/standards/indiana-academic-standards-{kindergarten,grade-1,...,grade-5}-mathematics.pdf
- **Official crosswalk:** IAS-to-CCSS Correlation Guides (updated July 2024), one per grade: https://in.gov/doe/files/{Kindergarten,Grade-1,...,Grade-5}-Math-2023-IAS-CCSS-Correlation-Guide.pdf . The 122 K–5 codes match between the standards PDFs and the guides. Our links follow the guides, with the departures listed below.

## Codes
- `grade.STRAND.number`, numbered without gaps: K.NS.4, 1.M.3, 3.M.4, 4.CA.6, 5.DA.2. Indiana calls the strands "domains":
  - **NS** Number Sense.
  - **CA** Computation and Algebraic Thinking.
  - **G** Geometry.
  - **M** Measurement.
  - **DA** Data Analysis.
- Rows by strand: NS 33, CA 40, G 15, M 26, DA 8. By grade: K 15, 1 15, 2 20, 3 24, 4 25, 5 23.
- "(E)" in the official text marks standards IDOE designates as essential; Mathness doesn't carry that flag. Process standards PS.1–PS.8 are skipped.
- **Collisions:** Virginia's 2023 SOL codes have the same shape, and Mathness uses them as sheet ids. IN 3.NS.4 is fractions (CCSS 3.NF.A.3b), but VA 3.NS.4 is money. IN 5.NS.2 is fractions as division, but VA 5.NS.2 is primes. Arkansas's 5.DA.1 also differs from Indiana's. This is why the sheet written for Indiana is filed as `IN.5.NS.4`.
- Lettered CCSS sub-parts became whole standards: 3.NS.3 = 3.NF.A.2b; 3.NS.4 = 3.NF.A.3b; 3.NS.5 = 3.NF.A.3d; 4.CA.6–4.CA.8 = 4.NF.B.3a–d; 5.M.2 = 5.NF.B.4b; 5.M.5 = 5.MD.C.5b.

## Against Common Core
- 122 rows: 48 same, 49 edited, 11 moved, 14 new.
- CCSS dropped (no 2023 IAS K–5 standard covers them):
  - K: K.CC.B.4c, K.OA.A.5, and kindergarten geometry K.G.A.1–3, K.G.B.5–6 (cut to one standard).
  - Grade 1: 1.OA.A.2, 1.OA.B.4, 1.OA.C.5, 1.OA.D.8, 1.NBT.C.5, 1.NBT.C.6.
  - Grade 2: 2.OA.C.4, 2.NBT.B.5, 2.NBT.B.6, 2.NBT.B.8, 2.MD.A.2, 2.MD.A.4, 2.MD.B.5, 2.MD.B.6, 2.MD.D.9.
  - Grade 3: 3.OA.A.4, 3.NBT.A.3, 3.NF.A.3c (now IN 4.NS.2), and the line-plot part of 3.MD.B.4.
  - Grade 4: 4.OA.A.1, 4.NBT.A.1, 4.NF.C.5, 4.MD.C.5–7 (angle measure), 4.G.A.3, and the prime/composite part of 4.OA.B.4.
  - Grade 5: 5.OA.A.1, 5.OA.A.2, 5.OA.B.3, 5.NBT.A.1, 5.NBT.A.4, 5.NBT.B.5, 5.NF.B.5, 5.G.B.3, 5.G.B.4.
- K:
  - Subitize patterned groups to 10 (K.NS.4, new).
  - Repeating and growing patterns (K.CA.4, new).
  - Time words and calendars (K.M.2, new).
  - Temperature in direct comparison (K.M.1).
  - Number words to ten (K.NS.2); compare numerals to 20 (K.NS.6).
- Grade 1:
  - Ordinals to 20 (1.NS.3, new).
  - Number patterns (1.CA.4, new).
  - **Penny, nickel and dime values** (1.M.3, moved from 2.MD.C.8).
  - Area and temperature in comparisons (1.M.1).
- Grade 2:
  - Order and grouping of addends (2.CA.3, moved from 1.OA.B.3).
  - Patterns within 1,000 (2.CA.4, new).
  - Composing and decomposing shapes (2.G.2, new).
  - in/ft/yd and cm/m relations (2.M.1, moved from 4.MD.A.1); cups and pints (2.M.3, moved from 3.MD.A.2); time-unit relations (2.M.5, new).
  - Pennies through dollar bills (2.M.6).
- Grade 3:
  - Numbers to 10,000 (3.NS.1, moved from 4.NBT.A.2).
  - Points, lines and segments (3.G.2, moved from 4.G.A.1).
  - **Coins and bills with $ and ¢, and affordability** (3.M.4, moved).
  - °C and °F (3.M.2).
- Grade 4:
  - Mixed numbers and whole numbers as fractions (4.NS.2, moved from 3.NF.A.3).
  - Properties of multiplication (4.CA.3, moved from 3.OA.B.5).
  - Parallelograms, rhombuses and trapezoids drawn with tools (4.G.1, new).
  - Measure to 1/8 inch or the millimeter (4.M.1, new).
  - Data in line plots, bar graphs and frequency tables (4.DA.1, moved from 3.MD.B.3).
- Grade 5 brings in CCSS grade 6 content:
  - **Percents** (5.NS.4, new).
  - Area of triangles, parallelograms and trapezoids (5.M.3, new).
  - Mean, median and mode (5.DA.2, new).
  - Fraction ÷ fraction (in 5.CA.6).
  - Also: right, acute and obtuse triangles, circles, radius and diameter (5.G.1, new); remainders (5.CA.2, moved from 4.OA.A.3); histograms (5.DA.1).

  - Some generous IDOE "same" notes were overridden on content (3.NS.2–5, 5.CA.3).

## Uncertain
- The research had no CCSS match for 2.G.2, 4.G.1 or 4.M.1, so they're kept as new.
- The grade 6 content (percents, area formulas, mean/median/mode) has no K–5 CCSS code, so the `ccss` field is empty.
