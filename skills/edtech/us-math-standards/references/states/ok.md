# Oklahoma (OK)

In effect for 2026–27: Oklahoma Academic Standards for Mathematics (OAS-M), dated February 2022 and implemented 2023–24. These are Oklahoma's own standards: the state repealed Common Core in 2014. Mathness models them as a crosswalk in OK codes (edition `ok`, 203 rows) with Oklahoma's own pre-K codes. No math revision is listed, so there is no next edition.

## Documents
- Standards PDF (Oklahoma State Department of Education): https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/osde/documents/services/standards-learning/mathematics/2022%20OAS%20Math.pdf
- OSDE Mathematics page: https://oklahoma.gov/education/services/standards-learning/mathematics.html
- OSDE New Standards page. As of 2026 it lists only social studies, health and PE under revision: https://oklahoma.gov/education/services/standards-learning/oklahoma-academic-standards/new-standards.html
- All fetched 2026-10-01.
- Common Core comparison: none. OSDE publishes no crosswalk to CCSS, so every ccss/kind value is a research judgement.

## Codes
- Grammar: `<grade>.<strand>.<standard>.<objective>`. Examples: `K.N.1.4`, `1.GM.3.2`, `3.A.1.2`, `4.N.4.2`, `5.D.1.1`.
- Strands (Mathness domain = strand):
  - N: Numbers & Operations.
  - A: Algebraic Reasoning & Algebra.
  - GM: Geometry & Measurement.
  - D: Data & Probability.
- No lettered sub-parts; one row per objective. Rows per grade: K 27, 1 32, 2 36, 3 38, 4 41, 5 29 (total 203).
- Skipped: the Mathematical Actions and Processes (MAPs).
- Typo: the PDF prints `3.GM 1.1` for 3.GM.1.1.
- Collisions: no exact matches with other Mathness sets. Do not confuse OK codes with Nebraska's lettered `K.N.1.a`.

## Against Common Core
- Rows: 203, of which 46 same, 93 edited, 39 moved, 25 new.
- New:
  - Ordinal numbers (`K.N.1.3`) and subitizing (`K.N.1.4`, `1.N.1.1`).
  - Fair sharing (`K.N.3.1`, `1.N.3.2`).
  - Patterns from K: repeating, growing, shrinking (`K.A.1.2`, `1.A.1.1`, `2.A.1.1–1.2`, `3.A.1.3`).
  - Calendar and time words (`K.GM.3.1`, `1.GM.3.2`).
  - Estimating sums (`2.N.2.3`).
  - Same-amount containers (`2.GM.2.3`).
  - Sorting and building solids (`3.GM.1.1–1.2`, `4.GM.1.3`, `5.GM.1.2`) and nets (`5.GM.1.3`).
  - Thermometer reading (`3.GM.2.7`).
  - 0.1/0.01/0.001 more or less (`5.N.3.4`).
  - Variables and inequalities (`5.A.2.2–2.3`).
  - Mean, median, mode and range (`5.D.1.1`).
  - Line and double-bar graphs (`5.D.1.2`).
- Earlier than CCSS (counted as moved unless noted):
  - Coins in K–1, money in grade 3 (`3.N.4.1–4.2`) and fewest coins in grade 4 (`4.N.4.1`). Making change to $20 (`4.N.4.2`) is counted as edited of 4.MD.A.2.
  - Skip-count by 2s/5s in grade 1 (`1.N.1.4–1.5`, counted as edited).
  - Rounding in grade 2 (`2.N.1.6`).
  - Numbers to 100,000 and 2-digit × 1-digit in grade 3; 5-digit add/subtract in grade 3 (`3.N.2.3`, counted as edited).
  - Input/output rules in grade 3 (`3.A.1.2`).
  - Angles in grades 2–3.
  - Cube-counting volume in grades 3–4.
- Dropped in K–5. Some of these appear in OK grade 6 (6.N.*, 6.GM.*):
  - K: K.OA.A.1, .A.2, .A.4, .A.5; K.G.A.3.
  - Grade 1: 1.OA.A.2, 1.OA.C.5, 1.NBT.C.4, 1.NBT.C.6, 1.G.A.1.
  - Grade 2: 2.NBT.B.6, 2.NBT.B.9, 2.MD.A.4, 2.MD.B.5, 2.MD.D.9, 2.G.A.2.
  - Grade 3: 3.NBT.A.3, 3.MD.A.2.
  - Grade 4: 4.OA.A.1–2, 4.OA.B.4, 4.NF.B.4, 4.MD.C.5, 4.MD.C.7, 4.G.A.3.
  - Grade 5: 5.NBT.A.4, 5.NF.B.4–7, 5.NBT.B.7.
  - Partly: 5.NBT.A.2, 5.NBT.B.5, 3.NF.A.3 (equivalence starts 4.N.3.1), 2.MD.C.8 bills.
- Judgement calls (ours):
  - Coin objectives are counted as moved from 2.MD.C.8.
  - `1.N.1.8` open number line counted as moved from 2.MD.B.6.
  - `3.A.1.2` counted as moved from 4.OA.C.5.
  - `5.A.2.2`, `5.A.2.3` and `5.D.1.1` match CCSS grade 6, so they are counted as new.
  - `2.N.2.3` counted as new, not linked to 3.OA.D.8.
