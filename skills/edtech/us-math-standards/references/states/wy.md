# Wyoming (WY)

**In effect for 2026–27:** the 2023 Wyoming Mathematics Content and Performance Standards (WYCPS), emended February 2025. They took effect 17 July 2024 and were fully implemented in 2025–26. They are a trimmed Common Core: about half of each grade was cut, and the rest is mostly Common Core text.

**In Mathness:** a crosswalk in Wyoming's codes, edition `wy`, year 2023, 66 rows for 63 standards, at `/wyoming/`.

**Next edition:** none.

## Documents
- **The standards:** "Math Ch. 10 WYCPS", the current reduced edition, published by the Wyoming Department of Education. https://edu.wyoming.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Math-Ch.-10-WYCPS.pdf
- **Ch. 10 Rules on Standards** (revised 27 February 2025): these incorporate the PDF above by reference. https://edu.wyoming.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/WDE-Ch-10-Rules-7.16.25_24898.pdf
- **WDE fact sheet:** says the State Board reduced the number of standards and did not edit the text of those it kept. https://edu.wyoming.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/WYCPS-Fact-Sheet.pdf
- **Cross-check:** "All Currently-Adopted Grade 1 Standards" lists the same reduced grade 1 set. https://edu.wyoming.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2025-Grade-1-Comprehensive-WYCPS_no-PLDs_public.pdf
- **No official crosswalk:** none was found to Common Core. WDE's resources site needs a login.
  - Wyoming keeps Common Core's domain and standard numbers, so the mapping uses the numbers plus a reading of each text against Common Core.

## Codes
- **Format:** Grade.Domain.Number, with an optional lowercase sub-letter and no cluster letter: K.CC.1b, 1.MD.3a, 2.NBT.1c, K.G.4.
  - Numbers follow Common Core's, so gaps mark trimmed standards.
  - These short codes look like Utah's and South Dakota 2026's but are a different set.
- **Missing parent:** 1.MD.3a and 1.MD.3b have no 1.MD.3 text of their own. The 2023 draft (SBE, 24 February 2023) printed 1.MD.3 with A and B bullets.
- **Sub-letters folded into the parent row:** those that only restate Common Core sub-parts. They are K.CC.4, 1.NBT.1/2/4, 1.G.3, 2.NBT.1 (a–b), 2.NBT.7, 2.G.3, 3.OA.8, 3.NF.2/3, 3.MD.7, 4.OA.3, 4.NBT.5, 4.NF.2/3, 5.NF.7 and 5.MD.5.
- **Sub-letters kept as separate rows:** K.CC.1a/1b, 1.MD.3a/3b and 2.NBT.1c. This is why there are 66 rows for 63 standards.
- **PLDs left out:** each standard has a "Proficient student is able to…" performance-level descriptor (the performance standard) that repeats it.
- **Pre-K:** none mapped; Head Start's goals are used.

  - Grade 4: 4.OA.A.1–2, B.4, C.5; 4.NBT.A.1 and B.4; 4.NF.B.4 and C.5–6; 4.MD.A.1–2, B.4, C.5–6; 4.G.A.1 and A.3.
  - Grade 5: 5.OA.B.3; 5.NBT.A.3–4 and B.5–6; 5.NF.A.1 and B.4–5; 5.MD.A.1, B.2, C.3–4; 5.G.A.1 and B.3–4.
  - The TSV header has the exact list. Sheets for dropped content still exist under Common Core.
- **New:** count back by ones from 20 (K.CC.1b).
- **Edited:**
  - Counting back within 120 in grade 1 (1.NBT.1).
  - "One less" in K.CC.4.
  - Decomposing three-digit numbers several ways (2.NBT.1c).
  - Money problems up to $10 (2.MD.8).
  - "Fluently" dropped from 2.NBT.5.
  - K.G.6 names its target shapes: squares, rectangles and hexagons.
  - Narrowed: 3.MD.7 drops rectilinear figures, 4.MD.7 keeps only unknown-angle problems, and 5.MD.5 drops additive volume.
- **Moved:** identify coins by value in grade 1 (1.MD.3b, from 2.MD.C.8).
- **Limits in cluster headings:**
  - Grade 3 fractions use denominators 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8.
  - Grade 4 fractions use denominators 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 100, and whole numbers go to 1,000,000.
  - 3.OA.8 states the order of operations when there are no parentheses (a Common Core footnote).

## Uncertain
- **K.CC.4 range:** its PLD says counting objects "in a range from 10 to 39", which differs from Common Core's limit of 20. The row follows the content standard text.
- **Moved or new:** 1.MD.3b is marked moved but could be read as new.
- **Mapping:** 2.NBT.1c is mapped to 2.NBT.A.1 plus 2.NBT.B.7 because Common Core has no stand-alone multiple-decomposition standard.
- **Judgment calls:** the "edited" verdicts for 2.MD.8, 2.NBT.5, 4.MD.7 and K.G.6 could be read as same.
- **Name:** editions.ts says "Wyoming Content and Performance Standards for Mathematics". gen.py says "the 2023 Wyoming Mathematics Content and Performance Standards (emended February 2025)".
