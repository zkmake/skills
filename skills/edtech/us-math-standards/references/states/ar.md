# Arkansas (AR)

In effect for 2026–27: the **Arkansas Mathematics Standards** (2023 K-8, Algebra I and Geometry; the year they reached classrooms is not recorded). They are Arkansas's own standards, with their own strands and codes. Mathness models them as a crosswalk: edition `ar`, 174 K–5 rows, page `/arkansas/`. The DESE page, checked 2026-10-01, still lists them as current. No next edition.

## Codes
- `grade.STRAND.number`, numbered within each strand and grade, with no lettered sub-parts in K–5. Examples: K.NPV.1, K.GM.8, 1.GM.7, 3.CAR.2, 5.DA.1.
- Strands, with their clusters (cluster names aren't in the code):
  - **NPV** Number & Place Value: Counting / Counting & Number Foundations, Place Value, Comparison, Fraction Foundations, Equivalent Fractions.
  - **CAR** Computation & Algebraic Reasoning: Operations & Properties, Problem Solving, Algebraic Concepts.
  - **GM** Geometry & Measurement: Shapes, Measurement Concepts, Length & Width, Perimeter/Area/Volume, Time & Money, Conversions, Coordinate Plane System.
  - **DA** Data Analysis: Charts, Graphs, & Tables.
- K–5 rows by strand: NPV 50, CAR 56, GM 57, DA 11. By grade: K 22, 1 26, 2 29, 3 33, 4 34, 5 30.
- Process standards (the Mathematical Practices) are not coded in the K–5 tables and are skipped.
- Collisions: Indiana uses the same `grade.STRAND.number` shape with a DA strand, so the same string can mean different content in each state. AR 5.DA.1 is line graphs and frequency tables; IN 5.DA.1 is posing data questions and fraction line plots. Oklahoma's GM codes have one more level (K.GM.3.1). Always key by state.

## Against Common Core
- 174 rows: 142 same, 16 edited, 12 moved, 4 new.
- CCSS dropped or not found: K.G.B.6 (composite shapes start at 1.GM.2); 1.OA.C.5; 2.NBT.B.9; 3.MD.D.8 (perimeter is in 2.GM.7 and 4.GM.7).
- CCSS narrowed: 3.NF.A.3c is not stated outright; 3.MD.C.7b–d is not spelled out in grade 3 (composite rectangles come in 4.GM.7).
- K:
  - New: ordinal words (K.NPV.3); subitizing 0–10 (K.NPV.4); time ideas with clocks and calendars (K.GM.7).
  - **Pennies and dimes** (K.GM.8, moved from 2.MD.C.8).
  - Fluency within 10 (K.CAR.4; CCSS K stops at 5).
  - Temperature in direct comparison (K.GM.6).
  - Faces on solids (K.GM.3).
  - Object and picture graphs (K.DA.1).
- Grade 1:
  - Skip count by 5s (1.NPV.2, moved from 2.NBT.A.2).
  - Count backward (1.NPV.1).
  - Expanded form to 120 (1.NPV.4).
  - Coin names and values (1.GM.6, moved), and counting same-coin sets to 100¢ (1.GM.7, moved).
- Grade 2:
  - Count by 1s, 10s and 100s, forward and back, within 1,000 (2.NPV.1).
  - Add up to four two-digit numbers with a total of 100 or less (2.CAR.3).
  - Faces, edges and vertices of prisms, cubes and square pyramids (2.GM.2).
  - Moved down from grades 3–4: perimeter of polygons (2.GM.7); time unit relationships, including days, weeks and months (2.GM.10); adding and subtracting time intervals in whole or half hours (2.GM.11).
  - Area by counting squares (2.GM.8).
- Grade 3:
  - **Four-digit numbers**: rounding (3.NPV.1); value of digits, reading and writing to 10,000, and comparing (3.NPV.2–3.NPV.4, moved from 4.NBT.A.2).
  - Fractions of a set (3.NPV.6).
  - Facts to 12 × 12 (3.CAR.2).
  - Parallel and perpendicular lines (3.GM.2, moved from 4.G.A.1).
- Grade 4:
  - Frequency tables and scaled bar graphs (4.DA.1, moved from 3.MD.B.3).
  - Area and perimeter formulas for composite rectangles (4.GM.7).
- Grade 5:
  - Line graphs, frequency tables and scaled bar graphs (5.DA.1, new).
  - Powers of ten in order of operations (5.CAR.12).
  - Decimal division is kept (5.CAR.4).
- Judgment calls:
  - K.GM.3 is edited on K.G.A.3; it could be new.
  - 2.GM.10 is moved from 4.MD.A.1; CCSS has no days/weeks/months.
  - 3.NPV.10 is edited on both 3.NF.A.1 and 4.NF.B.3.
  - 3.NPV.2–3.NPV.4 are moved, because CCSS stops grade 3 place value at 1,000.
  - 2.GM.8 is edited on 2.G.A.2.
