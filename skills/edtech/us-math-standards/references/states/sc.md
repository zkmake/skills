# South Carolina (SC)

In effect for 2026–27: South Carolina College- and Career-Ready Mathematics Standards, called the "2025 standards" (approved December 12, 2023; in classrooms since 2025–26; PDF "Emended July 2026"). These are SC's own standards, not Common Core. Mathness models them as a crosswalk in SC codes (edition `sc`, year shown as 2023, 164 rows; pre-K follows Head Start). No next edition is recorded.

## Codes
- Grammar: `<grade>.<strand>.<standard>.<indicator>`. Examples: `K.NR.2.2`, `1.MGSR.1.4`, `3.DPSR.2.1`, `4.PAFR.2.4`, `5.PAFR.3.2`.
- Strands (Mathness domain = strand):
  - NR: Numerical Reasoning.
  - PAFR: Patterns, Algebra, and Functional Reasoning.
  - MGSR: Measurement, Geometry, and Spatial Reasoning.
  - DPSR: Data, Probability, and Statistical Reasoning.
- No lettered sub-parts; one row per indicator. Rows per grade: K 18, 1 30, 2 27, 3 30, 4 33, 5 26.
- Skipped: the process standards (MPS.PS/RC/C/AJ/SP) and the "insight" text.
- Collision: 30 SC `NR` codes (e.g. `K.NR.1.1`, `3.NR.1.1`, `5.NR.2.1`) are character-identical to Georgia's crosswalk codes but mean other content. Always qualify by state.
- Some indicator text in the PDF was extracted by layout. The codes and wording were checked against the raw text.

## Against Common Core
- Rows: 164, of which 63 same, 62 edited, 27 moved, 12 new.
- New:
  - Subitizing (`K.NR.2.2`).
  - Repeating and growing patterns (`K.PAFR.2.1`, `1.PAFR.2.1–2.2`, `2.PAFR.2.1–2.2`).
  - Make 100 (`2.PAFR.1.4`).
  - Probability from grade 3 (`3.DPSR.2.1`, `4.DPSR.2.1`, `5.DPSR.2.1`).
  - Range and mode (`5.DPSR.1.1`).
  - GCF to simplify (`5.PAFR.3.2`).
- Moved earlier:
  - Coin names in K (`K.MGSR.1.1`).
  - Coins and $1/$5/$10 bills in grade 1 (`1.MGSR.1.4–1.5`).
  - Coins to $5 as decimals in grade 3 (`3.MGSR.2.1`).
  - Place value to 999,999 in grade 3 (`3.NR.1.1–1.3`).
  - Angles and lines in grade 3 (`3.MGSR.3.1–3.2`).
  - Quadrilateral hierarchy in grade 4 (`4.MGSR.3.2`).
  - Fraction as division in grade 4 (`4.PAFR.2.4`).
- Moved later:
  - Multiplying by multiples of 10 and 100 goes to grade 4 (`4.PAFR.1.2`, from 3.NBT.A.3).
  - K.G shape naming, 2D vs 3D and comparing shapes go to grade 1 (`1.MGSR.2.3–2.5`).
  - 1.OA properties, true/false and missing numbers go to grade 2 (`2.PAFR.1.2/1.6/1.7`).
  - Quarter-inch measuring to grade 4.
  - Weight to grade 4 (`4.MGSR.2.4`).
  - Composite area/perimeter to grade 5 (`5.MGSR.1.1`).
- Other changes: numbers to 999,999,999 in grade 4; circle and line graphs in grade 5.
- Dropped:
  - K: K.CC.C.7 comparing numerals; K.G.B.5/B.6 building and composing.
  - Grade 1: 1.G.A.2; 1.OA.A.2; 1.OA.C.5.
  - Grade 2: 2.G.A.2; 2.NBT.B.6; 2.NBT.B.7 (moved to 3.PAFR.1.1); 2.MD.A.2, A.4, B.6.
  - Grade 4: 4.MD.C.5–7 angle measuring (starts 6.MGSR.2.2); 4.G.A.3 symmetry.
  - Grade 5: 5.NF.B.5 scaling; 5.MD.C.5 volume formulas (6.MGSR.1.5).
- Partly dropped:
  - 3.MD.A.2 g/kg.
  - 4.NF.B.4 non-unit fractions (now 5.PAFR.2.2).
  - 4.MD.A.1 metric conversion (now 5.MGSR.2.1).
  - 5.NBT.A.3b.
  - 5.NBT.B.7 limited to whole × decimal under 1.
  - 2.OA.A.1 one-step only.
  - 1.MD.A.1 indirect comparison.
- Judgement calls (ours):
  - Coins are counted as moved from 2.MD.C.8.
  - `K.DPSR.1.2` counted as moved from 1.MD.C.4.
  - `3.NR.1.1–1.3` counted as moved from 4.NBT.A.2.
  - `5.PAFR.3.1` LCM is linked to 5.NF.A.1 (CCSS 6.NS.B.4).
  - `2.PAFR.2.2` counted as new, not as moved from 4.OA.C.5.
