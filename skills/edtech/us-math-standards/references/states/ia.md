# Iowa (IA)

In effect for 2026–27: the **Iowa Academic Standards for Mathematics**, adopted by the State Board on 29 April 2024 (document dated April 2024). The research found no separate implementation deadline. They keep all of Common Core's codes and add 10 Iowa-only standards in K–2, marked `IA`. Mathness models them as a crosswalk in Iowa's codes: edition `ia`, 158 K–5 rows, page `/iowa/`. No next edition.

## Codes
- CCSS form `Grade.Domain.Cluster.Number` (K.CC.A.1, 2.MD.C.8). All 148 CCSS K–5 codes are kept with the same numbers and content.
- Iowa additions insert **`IA`** before the cluster letter: `Grade.Domain.IA.Cluster.Number`. There are 10 of them, all in K–2:
  - K: K.CC.IA.A.1, K.CC.IA.A.2, K.CC.IA.B.1, K.MD.IA.B.1.
  - Grade 1: 1.OA.IA.C.1, 1.OA.IA.C.2, 1.MD.IA.B.1.
  - Grade 2: 2.MD.IA.C.1, 2.MD.IA.C.2, 2.MD.IA.D.1.
  - A parser must allow an optional `IA.` segment after the domain.
- Sub-points are bullets, not lettered codes, and are folded into their standard.
- Domains are CCSS's: CC (K), OA, NBT, NF (3–5), MD, G. Clusters are labelled Major, Supporting or Additional.
- Rows: 158 = 148 + 10. By grade: K 26, 1 24, 2 29, 3 25, 4 28, 5 26.

## Against Common Core
- 158 rows: 140 same, 11 edited, 2 moved, 5 new. No CCSS standard is dropped. The within-10 fluency part of 1.OA.C.6 was split into 1.OA.IA.C.2.
- K:
  - Count backward from 20 (K.CC.IA.A.1) and from any number up to 20 (K.CC.IA.A.2), both new.
  - Subitize up to 5 (K.CC.IA.B.1, new).
  - **The penny, and counting up to 20 pennies** (K.MD.IA.B.1, moved from 2.MD.C.8).
- Grade 1:
  - Counting and quick recognition with ten frames to explain adding and subtracting (1.OA.IA.C.1, edited on 1.OA.C.5).
  - Fluency within 10 (1.OA.IA.C.2).
  - Count backward within 120 (1.NBT.A.1).
  - **Pennies and dimes to 100¢** (1.MD.IA.B.1, moved).
- Grade 2:
  - Count and skip-count backward by 5s, 10s and 100s (2.NBT.A.2).
  - No 5 × 5 limit on arrays (2.OA.C.4).
  - Minutes, hours, days, weeks, months and years (2.MD.IA.C.1, new).
  - Nickels, quarters and dollar bills (2.MD.IA.C.2, edited on 2.MD.C.8).
  - Data from interviews, surveys and observations (2.MD.IA.D.1, new).
- Grade 4:
  - Shape patterns dropped from 4.OA.C.5.
  - Any efficient algorithm to 1,000,000 (4.NBT.B.4).
- Grade 5:
  - Partial products or the standard algorithm (5.NBT.B.5).
  - The **standard long-division algorithm** added (5.NBT.B.6).
- Many standards add Iowa notes, examples and "See Problem Types Table" references; these are treated as same.
- Judgment calls:
  - The coin standards in K and 1 are marked moved; 2.MD.IA.C.2 is edited.
  - 2.MD.IA.C.1 is new, though its minutes and hours overlap CCSS 4.MD.A.1.
  - 1.OA.IA.C.1 maps to 1.OA.C.5; its subitizing part has no CCSS code.

## Sheets for state content
- Redirects (OVR):
  - Counting back: K.CC.IA.A.1 and K.CC.IA.A.2 → K.NOS.A.3 (Maryland).
  - Subitizing: K.CC.IA.B.1 → K.NOS.B.8 (Maryland).
  - Coins: K.MD.IA.B.1 and 1.MD.IA.B.1 → 1.GR.C.6 (Maryland's grade 1 coin sheet).
  - Time units: 2.MD.IA.C.1 → 1.MG.3 (Virginia time and calendar).
  - Surveys: 2.MD.IA.D.1 → 2.MD.D.10.
- Fit-review links (extra.tsv): 1.MD.IA.B.1 + 2.MD.C.8; 1.NBT.A.1 + 1.NOS.A.1; 1.OA.IA.C.1 + 1.OA.C.6; 2.MD.IA.C.2 + 1.GR.C.6. (The extra.tsv entries for K.MD.IA.B.1, 2.MD.IA.C.1 and 2.NBT.A.2 repeat links the rows already have.)
- No sheets were written for Iowa alone.

## Uncertain
- There is no stated date for classroom implementation; the standards are treated as current for 2026–27 from the adoption date.
- The crosswalk and spreadsheet are Google Drive files linked from the guidebook, not files hosted on educate.iowa.gov.
